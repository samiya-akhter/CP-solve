## Problem Statement

Mushi-Mushi, our pro coder bhai, has been invited to his close friend’s wedding.
He plans to buy **exactly one** Punjabi and **exactly one** gift.

He browsed several websites and noted down:

- Prices of N different Punjabis
- Prices of M different gifts

Mushi-Mushi has a budget of X. He wants to know **how many different combinations** of one Punjabi and one gift have a total cost **less than or equal to** his budget.

## Input

- The first line contains five integers N, M, and X — the number of Punjabis, gifts, and Mushi-Mushi’s budget.
- The second line contains N integers — prices of Punjabis.
- The third line contains M integers — prices of gifts.
- **Constrains:**

1 ≤ N, M ≤ 50

1 ≤ X ≤ 1000

All prices are between 1 and 1000.

## Output

Output a single integer — the number of valid combinations where the total cost does not exceed X.

## Sample Tests

**Sample Input:**

```
3 2 60
20 50 70
10 15
```

**Sample Output:**

```
3
```
